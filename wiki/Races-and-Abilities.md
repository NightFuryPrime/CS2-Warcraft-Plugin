# Races and Abilities

This page lists every built-in race currently registered by the plugin. The role labels below are handbook labels for readability; they are not hard-coded game categories.

## Race roster

| --- | --- | --- |

| Necromancer | Sustain caster | Unlocked |
| Paladin | Support tank | Unlocked |
| Mage | Burst caster | Unlocked |
| Ranger | Mobility marksman | Unlocked |
| Rogue | Backstab assassin | Unlocked |
| Shapeshifter | Trickster | Unlocked |
| Tinker | Engineer summoner | Unlocked |
| Shadowblade | Duelist assassin | Unlocks at total level `50` |
| Dwarf Engineer | Builder tank | Unlocks at total level `60` |
| Death Weaver | Dark support | Unlocks at total level `70` |

| Sacred Warrior | Risk-reward fighter | Unlocks at total level `100` |

## Barbarian

Role: front-line bruiser with shotgun damage and a short all-in ultimate.

| Slot | Ability | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Carnage | Deal an extra `5/10/15/20/25` damage with shotguns. |
| 2 | Battle-Hardened | Increase your health by `20/40/60/80/100`. |
| 3 | Throwing Axe | Chance to throw an exploding barrel when firing, roughly `3-13%` depending on weapon. |
| 4 | Bloodlust | For `10s` gain infinite ammo, `30%` more speed, and constant health regeneration. |

## Choosing a first race

- Pick Ranger if you want the easiest mobility-focused entry point.
- Pick Paladin if you want stable team utility and simple passives.
- Pick Barbarian if you want straightforward health and damage gains.
- Pick Mage if you prefer flashy ability-driven rounds over raw gunplay.
